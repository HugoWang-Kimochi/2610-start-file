Top Seattle Attractions

Discover Seattle’s iconic landmarks, lively markets, and beautiful waterfront. From city streets to peaceful green spaces, Seattle offers plenty of ways to explore the Pacific Northwest.

Places to Explore

Start your Seattle getaway with these memorable destinations. Each place offers a different view of the city’s character.

  • Space Needle: Discover one of Seattle's most recognizable landmarks and enjory views of the city from above.
  • Pike Place Market: Explore local shops, browse handmade gifts, and enjoy the lively atmosphere of this historic market.
  • Seattle Waterfront: Take a relaxing walk along Elliott Bay and enjoy waterfront dining and views of passing ferries.

Enjoy the Outdoors

Make time for Seattle’s parks and open spaces. A walk by the water or an afternoon surrounded by greenery can bring a peaceful change of pace to your city adventure.

Parks and Scenic Spots

  • Kerry Park: Bring your camera to capture a classic view of Seattle’s skyline.
  • Discovery Park: Explore walking trails and take a break from the busy city streets.
  • Green Lake: Enjoy a lakeside walk or spend a relaxed afternoon outdoors.

Plan Your Seattle Getaway

Combine popular attractions with time to explore at your own pace. Leave room for a coffee break, a neighborhood walk, or a stop at a local shop along the way.

Before You Go

  • Bring a light rain jacket and comfortable walking shoes.
  • Group nearby destinations together to make travel easier.
  • Check opening hours and ticket information before your visit.
  • Allow extra time to enjoy the views and take photos.

Whether you are visiting for a day or staying for a weekend, create your own Seattle getaway through local discoveries, outdoor adventures, and memorable city views.
